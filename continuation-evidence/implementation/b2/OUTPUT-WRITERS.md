# Output writer inventory (report appendix)

The addendum adds an embedded `filesystemOutput` to File and Directory, with
`OutputRev dagql.OutputRevision` and `outputMu`. All four production accessor
writes are in `core/filesystem_output.go`: File.SetPath, File.SetSnapshot,
Directory.SetPath and Directory.SetSnapshot. Each advances the revision under
that object lock. No production call to their accessor setters remains outside
those methods.

The completion wrappers in File.LazyEvalFunc and Directory.LazyEvalFunc finish
under the object lock through finishLazyLocked. That helper retains the body
latch and producer, clears the operational Lazy pointer, and advances OutputRev.
Container's root/mount selectors call clearLazy for those same publications.
Stored-snapshot restore callbacks publish through SetSnapshot. Private decoder
construction restores producer fields, then advances OutputRev under outputMu
before returning the object. Composite-literal initializers below create private
objects before their first publication; an initial Lazy is retained by the
persistence guard. Attachment rewrites of lazy inputs remain covered by the
cache attachment barrier and its dependency revision.

The permanent negative peer is `TestCapturePersistedFilesystemDirectEvaluation`.
`TestFilesystemOutputRevision` checks revision advancement and a busy publication;
`TestFilesystemPersistenceRetainsBodyLatch` checks refusal after Lazy is cleared
while its body latch remains held. The Container direct-evaluation control also
passes. PersistedOutputRevision takes both guards nonblockingly; bodies never
wait for an object lock while persistence waits for their latch.
